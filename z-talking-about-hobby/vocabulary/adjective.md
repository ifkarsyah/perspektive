# Adjective

## Hiking

- Describing View
	- schön: beautiful
	- hoch: high, tall (superlative: der höchste Berg)
	- berühmt: famous (superlative: der berühmteste Berg)
- Describing Experience
	- anstrengend: exhausting
	- beeindruckend: impressive
- Color
	- grün: green
	- gelb: yellow
	- rot: red
	- weiß: white
- gesund: healthy
## Football

- lieb: dear (as in Lieblings-: favourite)
- gut: good

## Cycling

- gut: good (das Wetter ist gut)
- allein: alone
- schlecht: bad



**Adverbs for frequency and degree (not adjectives, but you'll use them a lot)**

- oft: often
- manchmal: sometimes
- selten: rarely
- meistens: mostly
- gern: gladly, like to (ich wandere gern)
- lieber: rather, prefer
- am liebsten: most of all

Two notes:

- **Colours and adjective endings:** you only need the base form when the adjective comes after the noun (_der Baum ist grün_). Before a noun the ending changes (_grüne Bäume_), which is the grammar we agreed to skip tonight.
- **allein** doesn't take an ending, so it's easy to use as is.
