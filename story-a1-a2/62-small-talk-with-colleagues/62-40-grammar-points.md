# 62 – Grammar Points: Small Talk with Colleagues

What this story adds on top of the topics written before it (`1`, `21`–`26`, `31`, `41`, `42`, `51`–`56`). The big ones: **opening and closing small talk**, **obwohl**, ***sollen*** **for "it's supposed to"**, **the *ihr*-form** and **ordinal dates**.

## New in this story

### 1. Starting small talk

| Question | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Na, alles klar?** | So, all good? | *Ja, alles gut. Und bei dir?* |
| **Und bei dir?** | And with you? | *Geht so.* |
| **Wie war dein Wochenende?** | How was your weekend? | *Super! · Ganz okay. · Ruhig.* |
| **Hast du was Schönes gemacht?** | Did you do anything nice? | *Ja, ich war wandern.* |
| **Ganz schön kalt heute, oder?** | Pretty cold today, isn't it? | *Ja, total!* |

How are you – from best to worst: *super · gut · ganz okay · **geht so** · nicht so gut*.

### 2. Reacting – showing you're listening

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| *Echt?* | Really? (surprise) |
| *Klingt toll!* | Sounds great! (`21` had *Klingt gut*) |
| *Das glaube ich!* | I believe it! (agreeing) |
| *Oh nein, das tut mir leid!* | Oh no, I'm sorry! (bad news) |
| *Gute Besserung!* | Get well soon! (`53`) |
| *Ich bin dabei! – Ich auch.* | I'm in! – Me too. |

### 3. *was* = *etwas* (casual)

In speech *etwas* often becomes **was**. With an adjective it works like *etwas Einfaches* in `24`:
- *Hast du **was Schönes** gemacht?* – Did you do anything nice?

### 4. *ich war wandern* – *sein* + infinitive for activities

To say you went to do an activity, use ***war*** + infinitive:
- *Ich **war** mit Lena in den Bergen **wandern**.* – I went hiking with Lena.
- (*Ich war schwimmen / einkaufen / laufen.*)

### 5. *obwohl* – although

Like *weil* and *dass*, *obwohl* sends the verb **to the end**:
- ***Obwohl** es sehr kalt **war**, hat es viel Spaß gemacht.*

Compare it with *aber* (normal word order): *Es war sehr kalt, **aber** es **hat** Spaß gemacht.*

### 6. *sollen* – "it's supposed to" (what you've heard)

*sollen* can mean that someone else says so – the forecast, the news, a friend:
- *Laut Wetterbericht **soll** es noch kälter **werden**.* – According to the forecast it's supposed to get even colder.

And ***werden*** **+ comparative** = to get … -er: *kälter werden · schöner werden*.

### 7. *ihr* – "you" for two or more people

| | kommen | müssen | sein |
|---|---|---|---|
| du | kommst | musst | bist |
| **ihr** | **kommt** | **müsst** | **seid** |

- *Guten Morgen, **ihr zwei**!* – Good morning, you two!
- *Übrigens, **kommt ihr** zur Weihnachtsfeier?*
- ***Ihr müsst** nur gute Laune mitbringen!*

### 8. Ordinal dates: *am zwölften Dezember*

For a date say **am** + number + **-ten** (1–19) or **-sten** (20+), then the month:

| Date | Say |
|---|---|
| 1.12. | am **ersten** Dezember |
| 3.12. | am **dritten** Dezember (irregular) |
| 7.12. | am **siebten** Dezember |
| 12.12. | am **zwölften** Dezember |
| 24.12. | am **vierundzwanzigsten** Dezember |

### 9. Closing small talk

- *Wir müssen **los**.* – We have to go.
- *Das Meeting **fängt gleich an**.* – The meeting starts soon.
- *Bis später!* – See you later!

### 10. Small verbs for joining in

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **dazu**kommen | to join (people talking) | *Dann kommt Julia **dazu**.* |
| **mit**kommen | to come along | *Vielleicht komme ich nächstes Mal **mit**.* |
| **dabei** sein | to be in, take part | *Max und ich sind **dabei**.* |

## Speaking chunks

- *Morgen! Na, alles klar?* – Morning! All good?
- *Geht so.* – So-so.
- *Geht es dir jetzt besser?* – Are you feeling better now?
- *Hast du was Schönes gemacht?* – Did you do anything nice?
- *Bei der Kälte?* – In this cold?
- *Es hat richtig Spaß gemacht.* – It was really fun.
- *Du bist immer willkommen!* – You're always welcome!
- *Ganz schön kalt heute, oder?* – Pretty cold today, isn't it?
- *Übrigens, …* – By the way, …
- *Ich bin dabei!* – I'm in!
- *Muss ich etwas mitbringen?* – Should I bring anything?
- *Wir müssen los.* – We have to go.
- *Bis später!* – See you later!
