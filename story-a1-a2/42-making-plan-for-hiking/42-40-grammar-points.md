# 42 – Grammar Points: Making a Plan for Hiking

What this story adds on top of the topics written before it (`11`, `21`, `22`, `23`, `31`, `41`). The big ones: **weather**, **things to bring** and **dates and deadlines**.

## New in this story

### 1. Weather always uses *es*

German weather sentences need **es** as the subject, even though "it" means nothing.

| German | English |
|---|---|
| **Es ist** kalt. | It's cold. |
| **Es schneit.** · Vielleicht schneit **es** sogar. | It's snowing. · Maybe it'll even snow. |
| **Es kann** schneien. | It might snow. |
| **Es sind** null Grad. | It's zero degrees. (plural *sind* with *Grad*) |
| **Wird es** nicht zu kalt? | Won't it get too cold? (*werden* = to get) |

### 2. *Hast du Lust, … zu* + infinitive?

The friendliest way to invite someone. The activity goes at the end with **zu**.

- Hast du Lust, mit mir wandern **zu gehen**? – Do you feel like going hiking with me?
- Short version: *Hast du Lust?* – Up for it?

With separable verbs *zu* goes in the middle: *Hast du Lust mit**zu**kommen?*

### 3. Giving your opinion with *finden*

**finden** = to think / to find (opinion). Often with the thing first.

- **Das finde ich** spannend! – I find that exciting!
- (*Ich finde das langweilig.* · *Wie findest du das?* – What do you think of it?)

### 4. Dates and deadlines

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mitte** November | mid-November (also *Anfang / Ende* November = early / late) |
| **im** November | in November (months take *im*) |
| am Samstag **in zwei Wochen** | on Saturday in two weeks |
| eine Woche **vorher** | a week before |
| **am Tag vorher** | the day before |
| **spätestens** | at the latest |
| **Bis wann** müssen wir es abschicken? | By when do we have to send it? |
| **heute Abend** | tonight |

### 5. Adjectives with no article in front

When there's no *der / ein* in front, the adjective itself shows the gender and case.

- **warme** Kleidung (die) · **gute** Schuhe (plural)
- mit **heißem** Tee (der, dative after *mit*)

You don't need to learn the table yet; just learn these as chunks.

### 6. More separable verbs, for planning

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| **mit**nehmen (take along) | Was sollen wir **mitnehmen**? · Ich nehme eine Thermoskanne **mit**. |
| **aus**füllen (fill in) | Ich fülle das Formular **aus**. |
| **ab**schicken (send off) | Wir müssen es **abschicken**. |
| **an**schauen (look at) | Wir schauen den Wetterbericht **an**. |

*mitnehmen* = take with you (from your side); *mitbringen* = bring with you (to the others).

### 7. Saying how sure you are

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **bestimmt** | surely | Es wird **bestimmt** kalt. |
| **vielleicht** | maybe | **Vielleicht** schneit es. |
| **sogar** | even | …oder **sogar** weniger. |
| **nicht genau** | not for sure | Ich weiß es aber **nicht genau**. |

### 8. Particles *eigentlich* and *gerade*

- **eigentlich** in a question = "by the way / actually": *Brauchen wir **eigentlich** eine Genehmigung?*
- **gerade** = "exactly, precisely": *Das finde ich **gerade** spannend!* (that's exactly what makes it exciting)

### 9. Short replies

- *Ja, die habe ich.* – Yes, I have those. (casual *die* = them, like in `41`)
- *Ja, machen wir.* – Yes, let's do that.
- *Ich mich auch!* – Me too! (reply to *Ich freue mich*)

## Speaking chunks

- *Hast du Lust, mit mir wandern zu gehen?* – Do you feel like going hiking with me?
- *Wann denn?* – When?
- *Wird es nicht zu kalt?* – Won't it be too cold?
- *Brr!* – Brr! (cold)
- *Was sollen wir mitnehmen?* – What should we take?
- *Ich glaube schon. Ich weiß es aber nicht genau.* – I think so. But I don't know for sure.
- *Ich prüfe das.* – I'll check that.
- *Bis wann…?* – By when…?
- *Dann haben wir noch Zeit.* – Then we still have time.
- *Sicherheit geht vor!* – Safety first!
- *Ich freue mich schon! – Ich mich auch!* – I'm looking forward to it! – Me too!
