# Recognize Only

## What is this about?

You don't need to **say** the things on this page. You only need to understand them when you hear or read them. So there are no exercises. Read through it, and come back when something looks strange.

Each section tells you what to say instead.

## Präteritum

This is the past tense of books, news, and stories.

### Regular verbs: -te

| Who       | machen    |
| --------- | --------- |
| ich       | machte    |
| du        | machtest  |
| er/sie/es | machte    |
| wir       | machten   |
| ihr       | machtet   |
| sie/Sie   | machten   |

If you see **-te** right after a verb's stem, it's probably the past.

### Irregular verbs: the vowel changes

| Who       | gehen  |
| --------- | ------ |
| ich       | ging   |
| du        | gingst |
| er/sie/es | ging   |
| wir       | gingen |
| ihr       | gingt  |
| sie/Sie   | gingen |

### Common ones

| gehen      | ging        | ist gegangen     | went    |

**Say instead:** the [spoken past](70-spoken-past.md).

## Plusquamperfekt

This means something happened **before** another past event. It's built with **hatte/war + participle**.

- Ich **hatte** schon **gegessen**. (I had already eaten.)
- **Nachdem** er **gegangen war**, habe ich geschlafen. (After he had left, I slept.)

**Say instead:** Perfekt with **vorher** or **davor**: Ich habe vorher gegessen.

## Genitive

This is the fourth case. It usually means "of" or "'s".

### The articles

| Article | masc.             | fem.          | neut.             | plural          |
| ------- | ----------------- | ------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| der     | **des** Mann**es** | **der** Frau | **des** Kind**es** | **der** Kinder |
| ein     | **eines** Mann**es** | **einer** Frau | **eines** Kind**es** | **meiner** Kinder |

Masculine and neuter nouns get **-s** or **-es**.

- das Auto **meines Bruders** (my brother's car)
- der Name **des Kindes** (the child's name)
- die Tasche **meiner Mutter** (my mother's bag)

### Adjective endings

They're nearly always **-en**: wegen **des schlechten** Wetters.

### Prepositions

**wegen, trotz, während, statt** officially take the genitive.

- **Während des** Essens hat er telefoniert. (During the meal, he was on the phone.)
- **Statt eines** Autos habe ich ein Fahrrad gekauft. (Instead of a car, I bought a bike.)

### Relative clauses

| Noun      | Pronoun    | Example                                     |
| --------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| masc.     | **dessen** | der Mann, **dessen** Auto kaputt ist        |
| neut.     | **dessen** | das Kind, **dessen** Fahrrad weg ist        |
| fem.      | **deren**  | die Frau, **deren** Kind hier spielt        |
| plural    | **deren**  | die Leute, **deren** Haus verkauft wurde    |

**Say instead:** **von + dative**: das Auto **von meinem Bruder**. And see [wegen and trotz](20-connectors/23-wegen-and-trotz.md).

You **can** say the -s form with names: **Annas** Auto, **Peters** Bruder.

## N-Declension

A small group of masculine nouns add **-n** or **-en** everywhere except the nominative. They're mostly people and animals, often ending in **-e**.

| Case | Form               |
| ---- | ------------------ |
| Nom. | der Kollege        |
| Akk. | den Kolleg**en**   |
| Dat. | dem Kolleg**en**   |
| Gen. | des Kolleg**en**   |

Others: der Junge, der Kunde, der Mensch, der Student, der Herr, der Name.

**Say instead:** just memorize the few at the end of this page. People will understand you either way.

## Passive in the Past

| Tense        | Form                   | English              |
| ------------ | ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| Präteritum   | Das Haus **wurde gebaut**. | The house was built. |
| Perfekt      | Das Haus **ist gebaut worden**. | The house has been built. |
| With a modal | Das **musste gemacht werden**. | That had to be done. |

**Say instead:** **Man hat** das Haus **gebaut**, or use an active sentence with a real subject.

Remember: in the passive, **worden** replaces geworden.

## Other Connectors

| German                    | English               | Example                                    |
| ------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| während                   | while                 | **Während** ich koche, höre ich Musik.     |
| nachdem                   | after                 | **Nachdem** ich gegessen hatte, ...        |
| indem                     | by (doing)            | Ich lerne, **indem** ich viel lese.        |
| ohne...zu                 | without (doing)       | Er geht, **ohne** etwas **zu** sagen.      |
| statt...zu                | instead of (doing)    | **Statt zu** arbeiten, schläft er.         |
| je...desto                | the more...the more   | **Je** mehr ich lerne, **desto** besser spreche ich. |
| entweder...oder           | either...or           | **Entweder** du kommst, **oder** ich gehe. |
| weder...noch              | neither...nor         | Ich habe **weder** Zeit **noch** Geld.     |
| nicht nur...sondern auch  | not only...but also   | Sie ist **nicht nur** nett, **sondern auch** klug. |
| sowohl...als auch         | both...and            | Ich mag **sowohl** Tee **als auch** Kaffee. |

**Say instead:** simpler connectors like **und**, **aber**, **dann**, and **bevor**.

## Memorize These Anyway

These come up so often that it's worth knowing them as they are.

### N-declension words

| Word        | Example                               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
