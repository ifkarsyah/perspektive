# More Connectors

## What is this about?

At A2 you learned to connect ideas with [weil, dass, wenn, ob](../../a2/50-subordinate-clauses/51-weil-dass-wenn-ob.md). That covers reasons, opinions, and conditions.

Now you'll add the words for telling a story and making an argument: **when**, **although**, **before**, **until**, **so that**, **that's why**, **anyway**.

- **Als** ich ein Kind war, habe ich in Jakarta gewohnt. (When I was a child, I lived in Jakarta.)
- Es regnet, **trotzdem** gehen wir spazieren. (It's raining, but we're going for a walk anyway.)

## The real question: what happens to the verb?

Learning what a connector means is the easy part. The real work is knowing what it does to the **word order**. There are only three groups, and you already know two of them.

| Group                | Examples                        | Verb goes...       | Example                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1. Don't take a seat | und, aber, oder, denn, sondern  | normal seat 2      | Ich bin müde, **aber** ich **komme**.     |
| 2. Verb at the end   | weil, dass, obwohl, bevor, bis  | to the very end    | Ich komme, **obwohl** ich müde **bin**.   |
| 3. Take seat 1       | deshalb, trotzdem, außerdem     | right after it     | Ich bin müde, **deshalb bleibe** ich hier. |

Group 1 is from [verb in second position](../../a1/10-present-tense-and-sentence-basic/14-verb-second-position.md). Group 2 is from [verb at the end](../../a2/50-subordinate-clauses/52-verb-at-the-end.md). Group 3 uses the same seat 2 rule you've known since A1. The word just happens to be sitting in seat 1.

## Why it matters

Without these words, you can only list facts, one after another. With them, you can tell someone what happened and why, and you can disagree politely.

- Choppy: Ich war krank. Ich bin zur Arbeit gegangen.
- Connected: **Obwohl** ich krank war, bin ich zur Arbeit gegangen.
