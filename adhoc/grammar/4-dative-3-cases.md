1. Verbs that give, tell, or show (two objects)

These usually have a receiver (dative) and a thing (accusative): geben, schenken, zeigen, sagen, schicken, bringen, erklären.

Ich gebe dem Mann ein Buch. (dative: the man, accusative: the book)
2. Verbs that take only a dative object

A small fixed group where the person is dative even though English would treat it as a direct object. You have to memorize them: helfen, danken, gefallen, gehören, folgen, antworten, glauben, passen, schmecken.

Ich helfe dem Mann. (I help the man. Not den Mann.)
Das Buch gehört meinem Vater. (The book belongs to my father.)
3. Prepositions (the part you already learned)

Dative also appears after prepositions, with no special verb needed: mit, zu, bei, von, aus, nach, seit, and the two-way ones when talking about location.

Ich gehe zum Arzt. (zu + dem)
Ich bin im Büro. (in + dem)
