# am, um, im

## The idea in one sentence

**am** for days, **um** for clock times, **im** for months and seasons.

## Step 1: The three main words

### am = on (days and parts of the day)

| German          | English               |
| --------------- | --------------------- |
| **am** Montag   | on Monday             |
| **am** Wochenende | on the weekend      |
| **am** 3. Mai   | on May 3rd            |
| **am** Morgen   | in the morning        |
| **am** Nachmittag | in the afternoon    |
| **am** Abend    | in the evening        |

Careful: English says "**in** the morning", but German says "**am** Morgen". Parts of the day use am.

Exception: **in der Nacht** (at night).

### um = at (clock times)

| German            | English          |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| **um** 8 Uhr      | at 8 o'clock     |
| **um** halb neun  | at half past eight |
| **um** Mitternacht | at midnight     |

### im = in (months and seasons)

| German           | English          |
| ---------------- | ---------------- |
| **im** Juli      | in July          |
| **im** Sommer    | in summer        |
| **im** Winter    | in winter        |

### Years: no word at all

- Ich bin **2001** geboren. (I was born in 2001.)
- Or the longer way: **im Jahr** 2001.

❌ Never say "in 2001" in German. That's an English habit.

### From ... to: von ... bis

- **von** 9 **bis** 17 Uhr (from 9 to 5)
- **von** Montag **bis** Freitag (from Monday to Friday)

## Step 2: Telling the time

**Wie spät ist es?** or **Wie viel Uhr ist es?** = What time is it?

### Full hours

- 8:00 → Es ist **acht Uhr**.

### Quarter past, quarter to

- 8:15 → **Viertel nach** acht (quarter after eight)
- 8:45 → **Viertel vor** neun (quarter before nine)

### Minutes past, minutes to

- 8:10 → **zehn nach** acht (ten after eight)
- 8:50 → **zehn vor** neun (ten before nine)

### ⚠️ The big trap: halb

This is the one that confuses everyone:

- 8:30 → **halb neun**

**halb neun** means "half **to** nine", NOT "half past nine"! Germans look at the **next** hour and say you're halfway there.

| Time  | German       | Literal           |
| ----- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| 7:30  | halb acht    | half to eight     |
| 8:30  | halb neun    | half to nine      |
| 12:30 | halb eins    | half to one       |

Trick: when you hear "halb X", **subtract one** and add 30 minutes. halb neun → 8:30.

### Official time (trains, appointments)

Uses the 24-hour clock and just reads the numbers:

- 20:30 → zwanzig Uhr dreißig
- 14:15 → vierzehn Uhr fünfzehn

This version has no traps. If unsure, use it.

## Step 3: Time words without a preposition

These words already contain the "when", so no am/um/im:

| German         | English                |
| -------------- | ---------------------- |
| heute          | today                  |
| morgen         | tomorrow               |
| gestern        | yesterday              |
| jetzt          | now                    |
| später         | later                  |
| morgens        | in the mornings (every morning) |
| abends         | in the evenings        |
| montags        | on Mondays (every Monday) |
| jeden Tag      | every day              |

Notice: adding **-s** to a day or time of day makes it "every": Montag → **montags** (every Monday), Abend → **abends** (every evening).

Also careful: **morgen** = tomorrow, **der Morgen** = the morning. "morgen früh" = tomorrow morning.

## Step 4: How often?

From always to never:

| German       | English      |
| ------------ | ------------ |
| immer        | always       |
| oft          | often        |
| manchmal     | sometimes    |
| selten       | rarely       |
| nie          | never        |

- Ich trinke morgens **immer** Kaffee. (I always drink coffee in the morning.)
- Er kommt **nie** pünktlich. (He's never on time.)

## Step 5: Time at the start = inversion

Starting with a time is very natural in German. Remember the verb stays in seat 2:

- **Am Montag** gehe ich zum Deutschkurs.
- **Um 7 Uhr** stehe ich auf.
- **Heute** habe ich keine Zeit.

## Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong              | ✅ Right             | Why                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| um Montag            | am Montag           | days → am                         |
| am 8 Uhr             | um 8 Uhr            | clock times → um                  |
| in Juli              | im Juli             | months → im (in + dem)            |
| in 2020              | 2020 / im Jahr 2020 | no "in" before years              |
| im Morgen            | am Morgen           | parts of the day → am             |
| halb neun = 9:30     | halb neun = 8:30    | halb = half **to**                |
| Heute ich arbeite.   | Heute arbeite ich.  | verb in seat 2                    |

## Summary

- **am** = days, dates, parts of the day. **um** = clock time. **im** = months, seasons.
- Years: no preposition (or "im Jahr").
- **halb neun = 8:30** (half to nine).
- heute, morgen, immer, oft... need no preposition.
- Time first → verb second → subject third.
