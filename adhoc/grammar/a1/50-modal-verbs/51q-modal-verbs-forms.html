Modal verb forms

Pick the right form. Some sentences test which verb you need, so read the English.

    How to decide
    1. ich and er/sie/es look the same and have no ending: ich kann, er kann.
    2. The vowel changes in the singular: können → kann, müssen → muss, wollen → will, dürfen → darf.
    3. wir, sie and Sie use the dictionary form.
    4. ich will means I want to. It doesn't mean I will.
    5. In shops and restaurants, use möchte.
    6. You don't have to = nicht müssen. You must not = nicht dürfen.
    7. sollen means someone else wants it, or says so.
