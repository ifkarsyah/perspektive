mein, meine or meinen?

Two decisions: the owner picks the word, and the thing picks the ending.

    How to decide
    1. Who owns it? ich → mein, du → dein, er/es → sein, sie → ihr, wir → unser, ihr → euer, sie (they) → ihr, Sie → Ihr.
    2. Is the thing masculine and the object? Add -en.
    3. Is the thing feminine or plural? Add -e.
    4. Anything else? No ending.
    5. euer drops its -e- when it gets an ending: eure, euren.
