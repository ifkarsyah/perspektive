der or den?

Fill the blank. Only masculine changes: der becomes den. die and das stay the same.

    How to decide
    1. Is the noun masculine? If not, the case doesn't matter: die and das stay the same.
    2. Who or what is doing it? That's the subject, so der.
    3. What or who is it being done to? That's the object, so den.
    4. After sein (ist, sind), there's no object, so der.
    5. Don't trust the position. The object can come first: Den Mann sieht der Hund.
