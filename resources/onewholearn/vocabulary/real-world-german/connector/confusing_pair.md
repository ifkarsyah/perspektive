# Most Commonly Confused Pairs

Source: https://onewholearns.com/vocabulary/connectors

These pairs share a meaning but not a grammar role — using the wrong one breaks word order.

## weil vs denn — both mean "because"

| | weil (subordinating) | denn (coordinating) |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Ich gehe, weil ich müde bin. | Ich gehe, denn ich bin müde. |
| English | I'm going because I'm tired. | I'm going, because I'm tired. |
| Rule | Verb to end | V2 kept |

## als vs wenn — both translate as "when"

| | als (single past event) | wenn (repeated / conditional) |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Als ich jung war, spielte ich Fußball. | Wenn es regnet, bleibe ich zu Hause. |
| English | When I was young, I played football. | When it rains, I stay home. |
| Rule | Unique past memory | Repeated or future |

## trotzdem vs obwohl — both express "despite"

| | obwohl (subordinating) | trotzdem (adverbial) |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Ich gehe raus, obwohl es regnet. | Es regnet. Trotzdem gehe ich raus. |
| English | I'm going out, although it's raining. | It's raining. Nevertheless I go out. |
| Rule | Verb to end | Inversion if clause-initial |

## da vs weil — both mean "because / since"

| | weil (neutral, spoken) | da (formal, written) |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Ich komme nicht, weil ich krank bin. | Da er krank ist, kommt er nicht. |
| English | I'm not coming because I'm sick. | Since he's sick, he's not coming. |
| Rule | Most common in speech | Formal / clause-initial |

## bevor vs nachdem — sequencing

| | bevor (before) | nachdem (after) |
|---|---|---|
| Example | Bevor ich gehe, trinke ich Kaffee. | Nachdem ich gegessen hatte, schlief ich. |
| English | Before I go, I drink coffee. | After I had eaten, I slept. |
| Rule | Verb to end in sub-clause | Often paired with past perfect |
