# Strategy to Retrieve

In here, we don't learn German. We acquire it.

Acquiring means **pulling German out of your head**, not reading it again. Every step below makes you produce or recognise German *before* you look at the answer.

## The loop

### 1. Pick the situation

What will you do in **1 hour, 1 day or 1 week**? Find its folder.

> Tomorrow I go to the Bürgeramt → `2-moving-in-and-formal-situation/2-bürgeramt`

### 2. Revise the phrases

1. **`phrase_to_hear.csv` first.** Read the German, say the meaning in English, then check. You can't control what they say, so recognising it matters most.
2. **`phrase_to_say.csv` next.** Cover the German, read the English, say the German **out loud**, then check.
3. Use the `who` column to pick **Sie** (staff, official, doctor…) or **du** (friend, flatmate, teammate…).

### 3. Run the scenarios

Act out the situation from start to finish, mixing what you say with what you hear:

- Swap the slot: "Ich brauche **einen Termin** / **eine Bescheinigung** / **ein Formular**."
- Plan for what could go wrong: "Das Formular ist nicht vollständig." What do you answer?
- Ask Claude to play the `who` (the official, waiter or bouncer) and talk to you in German.

### 4. Revise the vocab

Go through the `vocab_*.csv` files in this order, using the `similarity` column:

1. **false-friend**: these trick you, so drill them hardest
2. **different**: memorise these
3. **cognate**: just skim them

Test both directions: German → English, then English → German.

### 5. Debrief after the real situation

Straight after, write down:

- what you **wanted to say but couldn't**, and add it to `phrase_to_say.csv`
- what you **heard but didn't understand**, and add it to `phrase_to_hear.csv`

This is how the files grow from your real life.

## When to do what

| Time before | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 week | Step 4 (false-friend + different), then step 3 a few times |
| 1 day | The full loop, steps 2–4 |
| 1 hour | Step 2 only: all of `phrase_to_hear` + your 5 most important `phrase_to_say` |
| Right after | Step 5 |

## Keep it alive

Come back to a folder **1 day, 3 days and 1 week** after you last revised it. Each time, start by testing yourself, before you read anything.
